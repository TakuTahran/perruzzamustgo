Housing growth along Jane Street is becoming an increasingly important issue for Ward 7 residents.

One recent case involves 2775 Jane Street, where Toronto considered a redevelopment proposal involving new housing while also addressing protections for existing renters.

## Existing Rental Housing

One of the central elements of the project involved protecting existing rental units.

Toronto planning decisions often include rental-replacement requirements when older apartment buildings are redeveloped.

These protections are intended to prevent existing tenants from simply losing their housing because redevelopment makes the property more valuable.

## New Housing Supply

The proposal also involved hundreds of new residential units.

For a city facing a housing shortage, projects like this contribute to the broader debate about how much density should be added around major transit corridors and established neighbourhoods.

## Perruzza’s Position

Anthony Perruzza did not support the final Council position on the proposal.

That places the project among several development cases where residents can examine his approach to growth.

The important question is not simply whether he voted “yes” or “no.”

Residents should examine why he opposed the proposal, what planning concerns were raised and whether those concerns outweighed the need for additional housing.
