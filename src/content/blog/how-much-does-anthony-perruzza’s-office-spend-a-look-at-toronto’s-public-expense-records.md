Municipal councillors receive public funding to operate constituency offices, hire staff, communicate with residents and perform their official duties.

Toronto publishes annual reports showing how much councillors spend.

Anthony Perruzza’s office expenses are therefore available for public review.

## Why Councillor Expenses Matter

Councillor spending is not automatically controversial.

Running a busy constituency office requires staffing, communications, technology, transportation and other resources.

However, because taxpayers fund those expenses, the public has a legitimate interest in understanding how the money is used.

## What Residents Should Examine

A useful expense review should look beyond a single total.

Residents can examine the categories of spending, unusual purchases, travel costs, communications expenses and year-to-year changes.

Those details provide a better picture than simply stating that a councillor spent a particular amount.

## Spending Does Not Automatically Mean Waste

This distinction is important.

An expense appearing in a public report does not mean that the expense violated City policy.

To describe spending as improper, wasteful or unauthorized requires additional evidence.

However, residents are still entitled to ask whether individual purchases represented good value for public money.
