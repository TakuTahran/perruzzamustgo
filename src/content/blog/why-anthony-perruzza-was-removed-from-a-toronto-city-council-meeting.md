Toronto City Council meetings can become heated, but a December 2023 confrontation involving Ward 7 Councillor Anthony Perruzza went beyond an ordinary policy disagreement.

The incident ended with Speaker Frances Nunziata removing Perruzza from the meeting.

## What Started the Dispute?

The issue arose while Perruzza was participating remotely.

According to Toronto Council records, there was a dispute concerning remote voting and whether Perruzza’s camera was switched on as required.

Speaker Nunziata made a procedural ruling regarding the situation.

What followed became a broader confrontation over Council procedure and conduct.

## The Apology Dispute

The official record shows that another councillor raised concerns about Perruzza’s comments toward the Speaker.

Nunziata then gave Perruzza an opportunity to apologize.

The Speaker repeatedly asked whether he wanted to apologize.

After Perruzza did not provide the apology she requested, Nunziata exercised her authority as Speaker and removed him from the meeting.

## Was This an Ethics Violation?

No.

This distinction is important.

The event is documented in Toronto City Council records, but it was not an Integrity Commissioner finding and did not establish criminal or ethical misconduct under a separate investigation.

It was a **Council procedure and conduct incident**.

## Why It Still Matters

Council chambers rely on rules to ensure debates can continue even during intense disagreements.

When a councillor is formally removed from a meeting, it becomes part of the public record that residents can consider when evaluating temperament, professionalism and effectiveness.

For Ward 7 residents, the event provides another window into how Anthony Perruzza has interacted with colleagues and Council leadership during contentious debates.

It should be reported accurately: he was removed from part of a Council meeting following a procedural confrontation and dispute over an apology.
