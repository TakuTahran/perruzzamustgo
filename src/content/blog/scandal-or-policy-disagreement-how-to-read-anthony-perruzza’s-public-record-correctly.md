Political reporting becomes misleading when every negative headline is described as a scandal.

Anthony Perruzza’s public record demonstrates why these distinctions matter.

## Ethics Finding

The 2024 Integrity Commissioner decision involving election-period City resources represents an official Code of Conduct violation.

That is an established factual finding.

## Council Conduct

Perruzza has also been involved in procedural confrontations during Toronto Council meetings.

These incidents are documented in official minutes, but they do not automatically represent separate ethics violations.

## Policy Disagreements

Votes concerning speed cameras, parks or development belong to another category entirely.

A councillor may take a position that residents disagree with or that Council rejects.

That does not itself constitute wrongdoing.

## Why Accurate Language Matters

When news outlets use exaggerated terminology, audiences may lose trust.

A stronger approach is to tell readers exactly what occurred and allow them to judge the significance.

For Anthony Perruzza, that means separating confirmed ethics matters from ordinary political disagreements and Council conduct disputes.
